Privacy Policy

Pocket OTT Monitor · Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Türkçe

Summary

Pocket OTT Monitor does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. The developer (Onur Mesta, an individual) receives no data from the app.

What stays on your device

The channels you add, their stream URLs, request headers and encryption keys, measurement history and preferences are stored on your iPhone. Sensitive values (URLs, headers, keys) are kept in the iOS Keychain. This data leaves your device only if you choose to export or share it, for example a channel list, a report or a diagnostic file. Diagnostic exports redact secrets. Deleting the app removes this data.

Network connections the app makes

  • Streams you add. To measure a stream, the app connects directly from your iPhone to the stream addresses you entered and downloads manifests and media segments, as any video player does. The operators of those servers can see your iPhone's IP address and the requests, under their own policies. The developer does not see them.
  • Local network. The app may use the local network to reach a server you configured and to play streams through a media gateway that runs only on your device.

Optional: your own Pocket OTT Monitor server

If your organization runs a Pocket OTT Monitor server and you pair your iPhone by scanning its one-time QR code, the app exchanges data with that server: the pairing code, a device record, the push notification token if you enable notifications, and the channel and alarm information the server shows you. That server is operated by your organization, not by the developer, and your organization's policies apply. The developer has no access to it. You can revoke the pairing from the server at any time.

Push notifications

Notifications are optional and requested only after you pair with a server and enable them. They are delivered through Apple Push Notification service. The notification token is registered only with your own server.

Camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos

The app does not use them.

Children

The app is a professional diagnostic tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
