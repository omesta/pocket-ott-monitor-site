Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. The app works on its own; channels and history stay on your device.

Which streams can it measure?
HTTP(S) HLS and MPEG-DASH streams, including streams that need custom request headers, POST requests or AES-128 keys that you provide. Protected (DRM) media is measured at transport level only; the app does not bypass content protection.

A profile shows that a segment was not fully downloaded.
The device test stops when it reaches the time or data limit in Settings (default 300 seconds / 500 MB for all profiles together). Raise the data limit to measure large profiles completely.

What is the private server feature?
Teams that run their own Pocket OTT Monitor server can pair an iPhone by scanning a one-time QR code from the server's web panel. The phone then shows that server's channels read-only and can receive push notifications for broadcast outages. It is not available without such a server.

How do I remove my data?
Delete the app to remove everything stored on the device. To stop server notifications, revoke the device in the server's web panel.
